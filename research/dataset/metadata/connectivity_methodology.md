# Connectivity evidence and transition design

The bundled OSM structure points are explicitly tagged communication structures, not measured mobile signal. `osm_mobile_phone_tag_present` only means an OSM mobile-phone tag is present; it does NOT verify service operation. Generic communication structures may broadcast TV/radio. Only nodes are extracted; missing structures and untagged sites are excluded. No current-connectivity label or coverage map is supplied.

After an authorised OpenCelliD import, preserve radio technology, MCC/MNC, cell identity, estimated coordinates, sample count, creation/update timestamps, range and any documented signal column with its actual units and collection meaning. Do not assume averageSignal is valid dBm or a route-local measurement; consult the downloaded schema. Cell positions may be inferred centroids, not physical tower coordinates. No cellular export has been acquired in this release.

An academically testable feature is nearest **known compatible mobile-cell** distance in metres, stratified by operator/technology, with observation age and location uncertainty. This is a geometric proxy, not a probability of coverage. A propagation model would additionally need terrain, antenna height/frequency/azimuth/power and calibration; distance or line of sight alone cannot establish signal. No arbitrary distance cutoff should become an outage ground truth.

Prefer dated operator coverage rasters (with permission) and consented repeated trail measurements of service availability, technology, signal metrics, data-transfer success and timestamp. Collect different devices/operators, conditions and repeated visits. Record device and measurement method. Absence of crowd observations is missing evidence, not absence of network.

For the actual recommendation trigger, represent decision_time, current location, observed current data-connectivity, intended route, expected arrival times, lookahead distance/time, predicted connectivity and confidence for upcoming route segments. Download while current connectivity is observed and before an independently validated upcoming outage. Do not infer the current observation from a historical tower map. Validate transitions on held-out routes/operators/times using real measurements; report false missed outages, premature downloads, payload size and successful offline use. Current 2026 sources cannot validate 2021–2025 network conditions without explicit historical evidence.
