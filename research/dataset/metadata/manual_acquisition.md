# Remaining official acquisition steps

* Existing `knuckles_trails.geojson`: not attached to this request. Place a copy in `raw/trails/user_original/`; hash and validate it without overwriting. Compare with newly collected candidates; preserve original identity and tags.
* OpenCelliD: https://opencellid.net/downloads.php — create your own account, obtain an API access token, enter it on the official page, choose Sri Lanka if a current export exists. Never commit tokens. Keep the exact exported file, checksum, access date and schema; check CC BY-SA 4.0 and attribution at https://docs.opencellid.org/docs/downloads/overview. Exports cover cells seen within the last 18 months; a missing country export may require authorised API lookups. Crop by AOI plus a justified buffer. Cell coordinates are estimated locations, not verified physical towers or signal measurements.
* Dialog https://dialog.lk/node/453 and Mobitel https://www.mobitel.lk/coverage — request dated, operator/technology-specific raster/vector coverage layers and written academic reuse/redistribution permission. TRCSL https://www.trc.gov.lk/ is the official regulator to ask for public coverage datasets. Do not scrape map tiles without permission. SLT fixed home 4G is a different service and cannot stand in for mobile coverage.
* NBRO/NBRI https://lrip.nbro.gov.lk/portal/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=7524ad70c98e4124a6f4dfed6ea615fe and https://nbri.gov.lk/index.php?Itemid=208&id=38&lang=en&option=com_content&view=article — ask for Matale/Kandy and other relevant district susceptibility polygons/rasters, legend, CRS, version and licence. Obtain machine-readable data if possible. PDFs require georeferencing with control points, positional-error reporting, legend-preserving digitisation and independent validation; reference only until then. Do not treat susceptibility as event probability or risk without exposure/vulnerability.
* DMC https://www.dmc.gov.lk/hazard/hazard/Maps.html and https://www.dmc.gov.lk/hazard/hazard/Report.html — request licensed flood layers for the actual trail catchments. Available national profiles have limited basin coverage; lack of a polygon does not mean safe. Irrigation Department https://www.irrigation.gov.lk/web/index.php?Itemid=271&id=138&lang=en&option=com_content&view=article provides an official flood-map starting page. data.gov.lk returned a retrieval error; retry the catalogue manually.
* Meteorology https://was.meteo.gov.lk/api-docs — public CAP/RSS is key-free; JSON requires an X-API-Key obtained from the department. Ask for historical warnings, geometry, issuance/validity times and licence. The new site explicitly marks information provisional during development. Current RSS alone is not a historical hazard dataset.
* Safety content: use `raw/safety_sources/source_catalog.csv` official links. Obtain document-specific offline redistribution permission, or write original concise guidance reviewed by Sri Lankan clinicians and attributed to sources. IFRC 2025 guidelines are programme design evidence, not an already completed hiker manual. Seek park-specific wildlife, ranger and evacuation information from the relevant park/forest offices and verify contacts before use. Wildlife content is not yet sourced/validated.
* Visibility: not offered by the selected historical archive variables. Keep absent. Investigate the Open-Meteo Historical Forecast API https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api for issue-time-aware IFS forecasts and documented visibility availability; do not splice a different model silently into ERA5 data.
