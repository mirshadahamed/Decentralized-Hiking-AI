# Label methodology — no training labels supplied

This release contains observations/reanalysis and derived measurements, a target catalogue, and a validation plan. It contains no random labels, no purported expert labels, and no trained model. The catalogue mandatory flag is a proposed baseline design policy, not an observation or independently validated medical standard.

1. Raw data: OSM objects, API reanalysis responses, unchanged DEM tiles. OSM is volunteered mapping; reanalysis is a model-derived reconstruction, not in-situ weather.
2. Derived features: lengths, spatial proximity, DEM profiles and local slope. Preserve algorithm/version, CRS, source identifier and uncertainty. Missing data stays missing. No tower distance is yet calculated.
3. Rule-derived candidate recommendations: define versioned rules with guidance citations, contextual conditions, thresholds, rationale and abstention. Obtain Sri Lankan clinical/outdoor expert approval before using numeric thresholds. Cold risk must consider wetness, wind, exposure duration and clothing; heat risk must include exertion and vulnerability. High temperature alone is not a clinical outcome. Snakebite prevention is not inferred from invented encounter observations. Terrain slope is not landslide risk. No universal numerical thresholds have been asserted in this package.
4. Independent ground truth: experts assess representative trip scenarios without seeing candidate rule outputs, record recommend/do-not-recommend/uncertain for each resource, evidence and confidence, then adjudicate disagreement. Recruit first-aid clinicians, local guides/rangers, hazard specialists and telecom experts. Collect consented field connectivity measurements separately; do not invent outage labels.

Keep sample_id, resource_id, candidate_label, rule_version, rule_evidence, reviewer_id, independent_label, confidence, adjudication, and provenance in a separate label table. Blanks mean unlabelled, never negative. Mandatory resources can be served through a reviewed policy outside the classifier.

Split by entire route and weather grid cell, plus time blocks and held-out regions. Knuckles, Riverston and Meemure overlap: the same OSM way must not appear in train and test. Deduplicate by osm_type/osm_id across regions. Related points and adjacent hours cannot be randomly split. Hold test experts/scenarios separate from rule development. Report multilabel precision/recall, macro and per-resource performance, safety-critical false negatives, calibration, abstention and confidence intervals; compare against a mandatory-only baseline and rule baseline.

Evaluation against the same rules that generated labels only measures rule imitation, not independent recommendation validity. State this explicitly in research claims. Prospective usability/coverage studies and blinded expert validation are required for novelty and safety claims.

Critical temporal leakage: the system recommends BEFORE connectivity loss. Historical realised weather is suitable for climatology/exploration and retrospective scenarios, but using future realised weather as a pre-trip feature leaks information. Collect archived forecasts with issue_time <= decision_time and their lead_time for deployment-like evaluation. Likewise a 2026 cell inventory must not be silently treated as 2021 coverage. Record current observed connectivity separately from predicted upcoming connectivity.
